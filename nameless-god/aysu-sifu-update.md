# Aysu Harika · Sifu prestige, Gate of the Long Dusk, KÖS module

The sheet still runs on Nameless God rulings: no TL, no Morale, no Tempo or Feints, no armor overhaul (AC = 10 + DEX + WIS, uncapped), and temp HP keeps the higher value. The layout now follows the Covenant 1.9 sheets.

## Layout (Covenant 1.9 style)

- **Kit tab.** Every ability is in one searchable document, grouped by source: Technique, Stance, Prestige, Class, Origin, Equipment and Custom. You can filter it by action type and by source, and there is a Compact view and ★ pins. You can also add your own custom abilities from the Kit. It replaces the old Techniques and Traits tabs.
- **Tabs hold trackers.** Monk, Druid, Rogue, Sifu, Long Dusk and KÖS show their trackers. Each one has a "Kit →" link to the full ability text.
- **Turn buttons.** There are now three: **Start turn**, **End turn** and **End round**. The Rules tab lists what each one does.
- **New header trackers.**
  - **Coils:** +1 hit, +1 struck, +2 Parry, Five Phase chain dots, Hua Jin soak, and a Slack warning.
  - **KÖS · Swell:** Swell pips, Displacement %, mounted/grounded, waves, rain and Strain.

## Sifu (levels 21–26 active, 27–30 shown as "ahead")

- **Ki and hit dice:**
  - Ki max is 62, minus 3 for each bonded disciple.
  - Hit dice are d10 for Monk levels 21–26, which adds 12 max HP.
- **Calculator, on unarmed strikes:**
  - A Phase picker. It advances to the next generating Phase on its own.
  - A Fa Jin Coils field. Limb dice are multiplied by Coils + 1. Fa Jin is once per turn, plus once more on a Great Circulation.
  - A Long Death release toggle.
  - Touch of the Long Death Ki (0–20).
- **What happens automatically:**
  - Coil winding.
  - Generation, and Great Circulation (Coils double).
  - Listening (up to 3).
  - Seams (crit range).
  - Four-limb tracking for Unjointing.
  - Water's Deduct Days.
  - Seven Po.
- **On the Sifu tab:**
  - Paralyzing Strike (5 Ki, once per turn).
  - Burning the Capital (Hit Dice or max HP).
  - Five disciples with Carried Chain and Borrowed Force.
  - Four Ounces redirect, with +10% per Coil.
  - Cook Ding / Unjointing per target.
  - Before the Mover (10 Ki, once per round).
  - "Didn't move" for +3 Coils.
- **End round** empties your Coils (Slack) if nothing happened that round.

## Gate of the Long Dusk (21 and 24 active)

- **Last Breath** is offered when a target drops. You get temp HP equal to WIS + 10% of the creature's max, +3 Coils, and +5 Ki if it was a Water kill. It fades at the end of your next turn. It replaces the old Touch of Death temp HP.
- **The Ledger** tracks deductions per target and your own 10% column. Target max HP and Vital Damage use the reduced maximum.
- **Hour of the Long Dusk** costs 5 Ki, or is free after a Great Circulation. Each creature gets a fail or save. Desecrated creatures fail automatically.
- **Seven Po** has a pip track for each target. Po settle back at End round, and the seventh Po makes the target Desecrated.

## KÖS Spirit Module

- **Swinging.** The mounted maul is a calculator weapon. Pick the creatures in the arc, and each one gets dice + Displacement (8% + 1% per 2 Swell, max 13%). Each swing costs 2 attacks, makes a Shockwave and queues Cavitation (half dice + half Displacement). Cavitation resolves at End turn.
- **Ground and Sky.** Grounded while mounted halves your speed.
- **Shockwaves** have a list with fail/save damage per creature. Start turn moves them; they last 3 rounds. Interference works on 2 waves (×2) or 3 waves (×3 then ×2), adds +2 Swell, and stuns a creature caught in it.
- **Cloudbreak and the Rain.** Cloudbreak makes 4 waves. The rain goes through its stages, adds rain Strain, and at Flooding you take 5% at Start turn. A rain tick button deals 2% to hostiles.
- **The Narrows and The Closing.** The Narrows impales the target, with per-round ticks of 8d12 + 10%, rising each round. The Closing deals 30d12 + 25% and drops the target to 0 if it ends below a quarter.
- **Anchor, Breakwater and Mehter.** Mehter tempo steps run on their own, with the 4th-step prompt.
- **Strain** has three counters (Kös, Rain, Turning Year) and is applied to attacks and saves.

## DM rulings (applied)

- **Limb Dice +5 per limb:** Fists **10d6**, Elbow **9d12**, Knees **13d6**, Kicks **8d20**. Fa Jin multiplies these.
- **Sifu hit dice:** d10 for Monk levels 21–26 (+12 max HP).
- **Touch of the Long Death:** no CON save; it rides the unarmed hit in full.
- **KÖS and the combo:** every creature hit in the arc adds a hit to Ripping Rapids.
- **Weapon switches:** swapping between the trident, unarmed strikes and the hammer counts as a weapon switch.
- **Displacement:** damage multipliers (Edge of Pain, kill bonus, Vulnerable and so on) scale it.
- **Mehter:** stops after the 4th step. After you take the 8d20, Mehter ends.

## Tidepalm, Hollow Collapse, Persistence (DM additions)

These are in the Kit under Wanderer techniques. Their trackers are on the **Combo tab**.

- **Spitting Tide** is a calculator attack.
  - Damage: 5d10 + DEX.
  - Cost: 3 Ki, paid automatically.
  - Always counts as a weapon switch (+1 hit, +2 switch).
  - Marks the target **Soaked**, and adds heavy bleed in Wendigo form.
- **Walking Tide** (3 Ki)
  - Only one can exist at a time. It lasts 10 rounds and advances 10 ft at Start turn.
  - Each creature it enters gets fail/save buttons: 8d10 + DEX, half on a save.
  - Each catch counts for Erosion.
  - A "behind the back face" toggle adds +5 AC for three-quarters cover.
  - It can be detonated or stopped by a wall.
- **Hollow Collapse** (2 Ki, uses a reaction)
  - Fail/save buttons for each creature: 8d12 + DEX.
  - A failed save also gives 1 Erosion, Soaked and Deafened.
- **Persistence** (2 Ki, or 4 Ki enhanced)
  - One per weapon. It lasts until the end of your next turn.
  - Start turn reminds you that persisting strikes attack.
  - A hit deals weapon dice only, plus the Trait Infusion die. It adds +1 combo with no switch bonus and counts for Erosion.
