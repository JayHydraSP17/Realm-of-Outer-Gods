How character trees work. Each battle a character fights in earns them upgrade points, spent on their own sheet's Upgrades tab. The Overall track improves their systems; the two Builds add combat abilities. Taking a node on a rung locks the other build's node on that rung. A rung opens at a level, after enough points in Overall, and after a pick on the rung before it (either build, so hybrids are allowed). Taking every rung of one build unlocks its capstone. A Lesser Draught of Regression refunds a character's whole tree. Equipment upgrades are bought with materials such as Tarnite. ??? marks upgrades not revealed yet.
