Three consecutive hits with the claws and the mark sets. It lasts three rounds, two creatures can carry one at a time, and the enhancement extends it or places it at range for 10 TL.

She sees and speaks with the Wayward, souls that fell with regrets still attached. She can separate her soul and walk into Caprevi herself, and her body lies unconscious in the living world while she does.

Every soul in range reads as a colour, as plainly as hair or clothing, measured against her own. White is faint, yellow is substantial and dangerous in the ordinary way, red is towering and deadly to her. It exposes a sheep in wolf’s clothing and the reverse.

Abilities

Ghastly Visages action

One creature she can see makes a Wisdom save without knowing it was asked. On a failure she sees the faces of everyone it has killed or is haunted by, and those faces shout information about themselves and about the target. A success locks her out of that creature until a long rest.

Wave Dashing reaction · 3 ki

An opponent moves within twenty feet or ends its turn there and she closes. Two extra ki avoids opportunity attacks. It has to be taken the instant it triggers, the same way you would disrupt neutral.

Parry exclusive dynamic reaction

Hit by a melee attack, she spends a token to strike back. Beat their roll and her attack lands and cancels theirs. Miss and the attack goes through with twenty percent more damage on it. The token is gone either way.

Her own technique on top of it: parry with a knockback strike, Kick or Knee, to spend the approach they just paid for. It pairs with Hex of Binding halving their speed to erase a whole turn.

Transformation · 75 TL

The Witch of the Iron Veil

She separates her soul and lets the spirits of Caprevi bind her in spectral armor. The Iron Maiden is a prison and a conduit at once.

Form effects

  • She passes through objects, unless she is doused in salt.
  • Physical attacks cannot harm her, unless she is doused in salt.
  • Divine and magical attacks harm her normally.
  • She carries no weapons, shields or physical objects. Pale Embrace counts as divine, so the claws, the Witch’s Mark and the Waywire all stay online.
  • She counts as undead: immune to psychic, resistant to necrotic, vulnerable to radiant.
  • Flying speed equal to her movement, and difficult terrain stops mattering.

Paid in life, not load

Way of the Geibo

The Geibo guided wandering souls from the living world into Caprevi and helped raise the Guardian of the Realm. The Guardian fell, their work went with it, and the mantle is hers now.

The two laws

Geibo’s Toll passive

Every technique is paid in hit points as self-inflicted Soul Erosion. It ignores resistance, immunity, temporary hit points and damage reduction, it cannot be redirected or prevented by anything, and it never stacks erosion tiers on her.

Spiritual Overflow passive

Landing a technique returns Overflow equal to a quarter of the hit points she spent. It holds for a minute, caps at proficiency bonus times level, and spends three ways: added to a heal, subtracted from incoming damage, or added to one attack as Soul Erosion.

Pay a toll

The buttons take the hit points off the bar and bank the Overflow for you. Overflow caps at proficiency times level, which is 65 right now.

Ancient techniques

Infinite Palm Strike action · recharge 5 to 6

Any amount of HP in blocks of ten, minimum ten. Fifteen foot wide line.

Each ten hit points is one spectral palm. Every strike deals three percent of the target’s maximum hit points as Soul Erosion and pushes it five feet, and she can travel with it. After each strike the target makes a Constitution save: succeed and the barrage ends there and it moves half its speed, fail and the next palm lands. Undead save with disadvantage. Once a creature succeeds it is immune until the start of her next turn.

Astral Displacement 50 HP

Before she hits a living creature she can lace the attack with psionic force and cut a limb off the mind rather than the body. Arm: it drops what it is holding and cannot use that arm. Leg: movement becomes zero. Torso: disadvantage on attacks. Head: no reactions. It lasts until the end of the target’s next turn, she can use it up to her proficiency bonus, and undead and constructs are immune.

Geibo Seal action · 100 HP

A seal set directly on a soul within twenty feet. Constitution save or the target cannot cast spells, use divine abilities or hold a magical transformation. It rerolls the save at the end of each of its turns. One minute.

The Shroud of Caprevi

Other domains take you somewhere else. Hers brings the somewhere else here: a shroud of Wayward twenty feet wide and ten feet high that nothing walks out of.

Once a creature is inside it cannot leave until she relinquishes the shroud or it escapes by divine or otherwise unique means. Salt alone will not do it.

Blind to the Living passive

Anything that is not close to death and lacks truesight or better cannot see the domain, though it feels every part of the effect. Senses are not hindered.

Latching on Regret passive

Every living creature inside takes 2d8 Soul Erosion a round. Anything that goes Soul Broken in here rolls death saves or joins the shroud.

Peek-a-Boo

Hidden in Plain Sight

Aura Sight 6th · replaced

On her sheet this slot is taken by Ghastly Visages instead, which shows her the faces of everyone the target has killed or is haunted by, and those faces speak. Both versions lock her out of a creature that saves until she finishes a long rest.

Spectral Blades 11th · not yet

The blades phase through cover and armor. Once on her turn she gives up one unarmed strike and instead forces a creature within that attack's reach to make a Dexterity save against her ki DC. Failure deals psychic damage equal to her unarmed strike, success halves it.

Psychic Form 17th · not yet

Bonus action to turn her body into the same psychic energy her blades are made of, for one minute, once per long rest. Resistance to all damage, a flying speed of thirty feet, and she moves through creatures and objects as though they were difficult terrain, taking 5 force damage if she ends her turn inside something.

Where it stacks. Psychic Slash triggers on flurry hits that deal psychic, and Soul Knives lets her choose psychic on every strike, so the rider is always available. Blooded Edge under Croqur and Dreadful Strikes from the ranger side both ride the same hits.

Aura of Blanking 7th · online

A psychic field that interferes with thought. When a hostile creature within five feet takes a reaction, it makes an Intelligence save or the reaction is wasted doing nothing while its mind lapses and it forgets what it was doing. She has to be conscious for the save to happen at all. The range grows to ten feet at 13th and fifteen at 18th.

Tormenting Shadow 11th · online

Hitting a creature with her shadowed weapon or using Ruinous Touch on it deals an extra 1d4 psychic and forces an Intelligence save.

Failure: no reactions until the end of its next turn, and on that turn it picks one of a move, an action or a bonus action and gets only that one.

Success: none of the above until the start of her next turn, but the extra psychic still lands.

Mindstorm 15th · not yet

Resistance to psychic damage, and every short rest returns half her ruin dice, never more than she has actually spent.

She can also spend two extra ruin dice when Mindwrack fires off Ruinous Touch. Any number of creatures she can see or is aware of within twenty feet must then save against Intelligence or be stunned until the end of her next turn. Intelligence 4 or lower is still immune.

Psionic Mastery 20th · not yet

Psychic resistance stops applying to her, and immunity only halves her damage instead of stopping it.

On the Attack action she can replace an attack with a use of Mindwrack that spends no ruin dice. It deals no damage and cannot be paired with Mindstorm, and she still has to be able to touch the target.

At the end of each of her turns, one stunned creature she can see or is aware of within thirty feet makes an Intelligence save or takes 3d10 psychic.

Base duskworn milestones still ahead 12 to 20

Aura Improvements at 13 and again at 18 widen every aura she has. Blasphemous Aegis at 14 makes her immune to disease and poison, unable to be charmed, and resistant to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing and slashing. Invoke Malice gains a second option at 13 and a third at 17.

Where it stacks. Aura of Blanking and the Aura of Despair overlap on the same ten to twenty feet, so anything standing next to her saves at a penalty and risks losing its reaction for trying to do anything about it. What the world says about witches

Witches are older than the witches of Msiona, derived from the fae. Their power runs on hexes closer to ruin than to spellcraft, their potions are greater elixirs, and they were said to control mana the way dragons do. After the ancient species were driven out and the dragons dispelled, the gods held the Greater Atlas, and the gap between mana and divinity widened until mana itself read as a disgrace. The trials followed, and the world executed mana users at scale for fear that magic would outrun the gods. The gods did not stop their followers. Witches disappeared into rumour, and their reappearance is always singular: one at a time, and nobody agrees where the power comes from.

Effacement. Western Arcadia, the Pale March, punishes by erasure: every record cut, the pages removed from the Atlas of Names.
