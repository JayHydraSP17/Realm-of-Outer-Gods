Weapon — Sylvan's Oblige

Current State Cycle: Spring — next strike has 25% to deal double damage

Sylvan Bow 1 Weapon Slot

Always lets Beatrix shoot three arrows at any target in range.

  • Dmg 2d8 · Range 80/320

Sylvan Blades 2 Weapon Slots

Dual-blades. When both strikes hit a creature, gain temp HP = 20% of the target's max HP (once per creature per round).

  • Dmg 1d10 · Range 5 ft · multiattack
  • Needs a free hand (weapon slot) to switch to blades.

Natural Cycle Bonus Action

Switch weapon state for a seasonal effect:

  • Spring (→ Bow): next strike has 25% chance to deal double damage.
  • Autumn (→ Blades): next strike has 25% chance to heal her proportional to the damage.

Coated Arrows Free Action

Coat up to 3 arrows with potions/poisons that have the Contact property. On hit, the target follows the rules of the coating or suffers its effect.

Passives

Alchemy Genius Passive

Add her Intelligence modifier to damage & healing of all crafted alchemical creations.

  • Rare and below: created as a bonus action — crafting time ignored, no check.
  • Above Rare: requires an action — half the checks, one round of concentration.

Alchemy Bag Passive · Daily

Each day her bag refills with:

  • 5 glass vials · 5 glass flasks
  • 2 common Reactive reagents
  • 1 common Curative or Poisonous reagent

Use Reset Daily Bag on the Harvest tab to refill.

Reactions

Leafy Torrent Reaction · 1/turn

When targeted by a melee attack, unleash three parts:

  • Spin — switch to Blades, whirlwind strike at everyone within 5 ft.
  • Jump — leap 5 ft up, reposition anywhere within 10 ft.
  • Shoot — switch to Bow, rain the trishot on one or more creatures.

Thorny Approach Reaction

When a creature enters within 20 ft, fire a thorny arrow. On hit: normal weapon damage and its speed becomes 0 until end of the current turn.

  • Uses = Proficiency Bonus

Bloom & Wither Reaction

  • Bloom (Blades hit): bloom a flower on the skin, max 3 per creature. Each flower gives +2 hit chance.
  • Wither (Bow hit): wither a flower to add +1d8 to that arrow's damage.

Materials & Quality

  • Reagents — alchemy ingredients bought, grown via Herbology, or harvested. Three categories: Reactive, Poisonous, Curative.
  • Essences — magical material from beasts, spellcasters, monstrosities. Categories: Arcane, Divine, Primal, Psionic.
  • Quality — Common · Uncommon · Rare · Very Rare · Legendary.

Core Rules

Blooming Vine Medium plant

  • Duration 10 mins, or until reduced to 0 HP
  • AC 10 + INT mod · HP 5 + INT mod + Inventor level · Speed 0
  • Can take one action; may use Dodge or OA reactions
  • Up to INT modifier vines · must be ≥10 ft apart
  • May expend a 1st-level+ spell slot instead → +5 × SS level HP

Tangle — each foot of movement away from within 5 ft costs an extra foot. Thorns — melee attackers within 5 ft take 1d4 piercing.

  • Lash (melee, reach 10 ft): 1d8 + INT slashing
  • Thorn Launch (ranged 60/180): 1d6 + INT piercing

Vine Abilities

Harvesting Vines (Passive) — once per round, each vine rolls the d100 Gather Reagents table; reagents store in the vine. While within reach, Beatrix may collect them as a free action.

Overharvest (Bonus, 1/turn) — an unused vine harvests again (roll again, take rewards). That vine is inactive until the start of Beatrix's next turn.

Overgrown Roots (Reaction) — a creature entering reach makes a Dex save or is restrained.

Verdant Bloomvine (Free, 1/turn) — if a vine has been out ≥3 rounds with ≥2 stored reagents, evolve it: HP +20 × stored reagents, harvest +1, damage die +1, AC +3, reach +10 ft. Evolving sacrifices all stored reagents.

Blooming Forest Passive

  • On entering, all Blooming Vines on the field evolve into Verdant Bloomvines — each gains +20 HP per stored reagent and keeps its reagents.
  • For each vine evolved this way, Beatrix gains +8 temp HP (max value of the 1d8).
  • Beatrix can plant two Blooming Vines instead of one.

One with Nature Passive

  • Becomes vulnerable to fire; immune to Blinded.
  • Can use the Vines as her sight.

Sylvan Judgement Passive

  • Switch weapon states as a free action (1/turn).
  • Natural Cycle becomes passive — always triggers on weapon switch.
  • Bloom & Wither becomes passive: Bloom forms every 2nd consecutive blade hit; Wither triggers on next arrow strike with reduced crit chance per active flower.

Interlocking Roots 10 TL Bonus Action

Attach the roots of vines together (toggle Interlock on each vine card). Attached vines gain:

  • +1 AC per vine · Reagents harvested +1
  • Beatrix's arrows travel through the roots (extended range)
  • Reagents move freely between attached vines
  • When one takes damage, all attached vines take half of it.

Toggle Interlock on each vine card — AC bonus and shared damage (via the HP − button) apply automatically across the linked network.

My Baby 10+ TL Reaction

When a vine is about to die, sacrifice TL to keep it alive at half HP. First use costs 10 TL, increasing by 10 per use. Resets after a long rest.

Next use cost: 10 TL · used 0× this rest. Trigger from a vine's ♥ My Baby button.
