Twin Gales — The Fans

Her paired fans. Track how many she currently holds — several abilities require at least one, and Deafening Winds sacrifices them.

Fans Held

Twin Gales Dual-Weapon Thrown 30 ft

  • Conduit. Used as her spellcasting focus, and usable for Spell Strikes.
  • Thrown. Range 30 ft.
  • Dual-Nature. For Spellstrikes, Magnolia can choose to infuse the spell in one or both fans.

Abilities

Aeromancer Passive

When using the Twin Gales to cast a spell using Wind or Air, choose one effect:

  • Increase spell save DC by PB (+7)
  • Increase damage roll by PB (+7)
  • Increase range by 5 ft × PB (+35 ft)

Wind/Air spells are flagged with the ≈ Aero badge on the Spell List.

Winds of Change Reaction

When a creature attacks Magnolia (or another creature within 5 ft), the attacker makes a Str saving throw.

  • On a failure, the attacker must change their target to another within 5 ft, or miss entirely.
  • Requires at least one fan.

Flirtatious Reaction

When a creature she can see (and that can be Charmed) tries to attack her, roll Persuasion vs the creature's Wis save.

  • On a failure, it cannot target nor move closer to Magnolia until the start of its next turn.

Deafening Winds Bonus Action

Hurl one or both fans at creatures within 60 ft. Each target makes a Con save or is wrapped in swirling winds until the start of Magnolia's next turn.

  • Silenced — cannot speak or provide verbal components.
  • Disadvantage on Con saves to maintain concentration.
  • A 5 ft area around the target fills with roaring wind; outside sound is muffled to those within.
  • Must sacrifice one or both fans to use.

Ride the Winds Bonus Action

Reposition anywhere within 30 ft as if flying with the wind. Requires at least one fan.

Enhancement (10 TL): Increases to 60 ft and can take one willing creature.
