DYLAN FLAGS DOWN VICTORY!

Flag of Treason (Core Mechanic)

A physical banner Dylan uses to ward off enemies and shield allies. Once per turn, as a free action, he plants the flag to release a 20 ft aura chosen from the chart. The aura's effect depends on which way the flag faces — the left side invigorates allies, the right side condemns enemies. While an aura is active Dylan carries its negative trait.

  • As a bonus action, recall the flag to his position.
  • The flag can be attacked but never destroyed (AC = Dylan). Damage dealt to the flag deals half to Dylan.

Plant, flip facing, and read the live effect in the Aura Engine tab.

Actions

Flagevin Throw Action

Throw the flag up to 5 ft × STR, planting it where it lands.

  • If it lands directly on a creature, they make a DEX save or are Impaled — taking 3d20 piercing damage.

Bonus Actions

Flagbearer's Arrival Bonus

Teleport to his flag without provoking opportunity attacks.

Look, I Got One! Bonus

  • Attempt to tether a creature to the flag — they roll a DEX save or are tethered.
  • A tethered creature cannot leave the flag's radius; at the end of its turn it may roll a STR save to escape.
  • Only one creature can be tethered per flag.

Betraying Myself Bonus Draft values

Dylan turns the banner against its own bearer, trading his own blood for the flag's power.

  • He takes 25 damage that cannot be reduced or prevented. This counts as Dylan taking damage for all of his aura effects (Veined Ledger, etc.).
  • Then choose one:
  • Regain TL equal to the damage taken.
  • Double the active aura's live effect until the start of his next turn.

Numbers are a starting draft — tune the self-damage and payoff to taste.

Reactions

Where You Goin'? Reaction

If a creature within 10 ft tries to move away from Dylan, he can attempt to grab them. The creature makes a STR save — on failure it is grappled in a chokehold.

No, I'm Dylan Reaction

When someone tries to Frighten Dylan and he succeeds the check, he can revert it back to the caster — or to another creature within 20 ft.

Better Off Alone Reaction

While Dylan is within his flag's radius, if an enemy or effect would target one of his allies, he can force that ally 10 ft outside the flag range.

Enhancement (10 TL) — Instead teleport the ally to another flag and grant them +2 AC.

Unbreakable Semaphore (Passive)

Once per turn, as a free action, Dylan can change the side the main banner faces (set it in Flag Markers below). Death-flags don't reflect or endure — they project his aura instead.

  • ◄ Reflect (Left-Facing): all long-ranged attacks — spells, AoE, thrown weapons — are reversed back at the attacker.
  • Endure (Right-Facing) ►: attacks are endured into the pool below. He takes no damage until the pool exceeds capacity; overflow spills to HP and burns TL. He cannot flip a flag until the pool is expelled or absorbed.

Flag Markers

Every flag on the field — the main banner plus any Ceremonious End death-flags — and what each is facing and doing.

Ceremonious End (Passive)

When Dylan kills an enemy in this form, a flag is planted where they die (AC 18 · 50 HP, both editable). While that flag stands, resurrection is impossible and it acts as a mini-flag conduit that projects Dylan's active aura — set each one's facing (allies or enemies) in Flag Markers above.

Unforgivable Sensation (Passive)

Dylan's strikes leave marks beyond description. Whenever he hits an opponent, they lose 10 Morale. If that drop knocks the opponent down a tier of Morale, Dylan gains an extra action.

  • Extra actions do not bank — any extra action not used is lost at the end of his turn.

The Flag of Treason — Conduit

The heart of Dylan's kit: a banner that can be planted, thrown, recalled, and tethered to. It cannot be destroyed — its AC equals Dylan's, and half of any damage it suffers is passed to him.

  • Plant (free action, 1/turn): release a 20 ft aura — see the Aura Engine.
  • Recall (bonus action): return the flag to Dylan.
  • Flagevin Throw (action): hurl up to 5 ft × STR; direct hit impales for 3d20 piercing.
  • Conduit: tethering and teleport features key off the flag's position.

A mantle worn by one who would rather survive than be remembered fondly.
