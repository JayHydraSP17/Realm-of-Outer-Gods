Identity

Melissa fights like the eye of a storm — she dances out of reach, banks Flow on every dodge, and brands Seals of the Ancients onto allies and enemies to rewrite their cores over time. Her seals drain a resource each round and climb through six escalating tiers; the longer a fight runs, the more inevitable she becomes. Every whiff and failed save the enemy throws is fuel.

At a Glance

  • Seal placement — on a hit, use a reaction to seal a creature (self-seal as a bonus action). Max seals on field = PB.
  • Seal drain — WIS × PB of the target's school resource at the start of its turn; every 3rd drain tick advances the seal one tier (max 6).
  • Failure = fuel — a sealed creature that misses her or fails a save she forces takes an extra drain tick (Eye of the Storm), and she can convert that failure into movement (Rushing Current).
  • Stays standing — Still Water converts a hit into a dodge; Waking Tiger plants a seal off a clean dodge.

Seals of the Ancients Core Mechanic

Place a seal, pick its school, and advance it with drain ticks. Every 3rd tick climbs a tier (max 6); effects are cumulative. Drain and progression scale off PB & WIS in the vitals bar.

Seals on field: 0 / 4
No seals placed. Use + Place Seal to brand a target.

Core Rules revised for functional sense

Placement & Cap

  • On a successful hit, use your reaction to place a Seal (1 per creature).
  • Place a seal on yourself as a bonus action.
  • Max seals on field = PB. Ally buffs share this cap with enemy seals.

Drain & Progression revised

  • A drain tick = one drain of WIS×PB from the seal's school resource, taken at the start of the sealed creature's turn.
  • The seal advances one tier every 3rd drain tick (Tier 6 at 15 ticks). Effects are cumulative.
  • If the resource hits 0 with no alternative, the tick instead deals WIS×PB necrotic.

Note: the original draft stated progression two ways (a "total threshold" and "every 3rd tick"). This sheet uses the single rule above — the threshold is three ticks. The 0-resource fallback was also raised to WIS×PB to match the drain.

Passives

Read the Flow Passive

Each round, the first time Melissa dodges an attack, she gains advantage on her next action against that enemy.

Flow State Passive

After every successful dodge, gain 1 Flow (max PB). Per stack: +2 Dodge checks, +5 ft movement. Lose 1 stack each time she is hit.

Eye of the Storm Passive clarified

When a sealed creature misses her or fails a save she forces, its seal takes an immediate +1 drain tick (applying the drain and counting toward tier progression).

Bonus Action

Undertow Bonus Action 2 Ki

Once per round, every sealed creature within 20 ft gains +1 drain tick, and Melissa may move up to her Flow movement bonus.

Reactions

Rushing Current Reaction 1 Ki

Once per round, when a sealed creature fails a saving throw or has an attack miss, immediately use Step of the Wind to move away from or toward the target.

Still Water Reaction clarified

Once per round, when Melissa would be hit she may spend 1 Flow to convert it into a successful dodge.

  • The converted dodge counts as a successful dodge for Read the Flow and Waking Tiger…
  • …but does not grant a Flow stack (you spent one to fuel it — net −1 Flow).

Waking Tiger Reaction clarified

On a successful dodge, Melissa may place a seal on the creature.

  • This placement costs no extra reaction (it rides on the dodge) and triggers once per turn.
  • Still bound by 1 seal per creature and the PB field cap.

Fighter Maneuvers Class Feature · unchanged

  • Superiority Die 5d10 · Save DC 8 + Dex + PB

Disarming Attack

On a hit, expend one Superiority Die and add it to the damage. The target makes a Strength save or drops one object of your choice, landing in its space.

Trip Attack

On a weapon or unarmed hit, expend one die and add it to the damage. A Large-or-smaller target makes a Strength save or is knocked Prone.

Evasive Footwork

As a bonus action, expend one die and take the Disengage action; add the die to your AC until the start of your next turn.

Maneuvering Attack

On a hit, expend one die (added to damage) and choose a willing ally who can see/hear you. They may use their reaction to move up to half their Speed without provoking an opportunity attack from your target.

Distracting Strike

On a hit, expend one die (added to damage). The next attack against the target by another attacker has advantage if made before the start of your next turn.

Sweeping Attack

On a melee hit, expend one die. Choose another creature within 5 ft of the target and within reach; if the attack roll would also hit it, it takes damage equal to the die (same type as the original).

Lunging Attack

As a bonus action, expend one die and take the Dash action. If you move at least 5 ft in a straight line before a melee hit on this turn's Attack action, add the die to that attack's damage.
