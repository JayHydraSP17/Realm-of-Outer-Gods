The Sephopine Flute

A family heirloom that lets Sika influence others through song — at the cost of her mental fortitude.

Mental Power — MP Resource

Instead of TL, Sika uses Mental Power (MP): her capacity to channel her melodies without her mind shattering.

  • Regain all MP on a long rest, and half MP on a short rest.
  • Certain events may restore bonus MP.
  • Starting or switching a Melody costs 5 MP.
Melody Factor = (PB + CHA + WIS) × 2. The guide lists Sika's current Factor as 44. The PB / CHA / WIS split in the vitals bar is editable — set it to her true stats so every Factor-based effect scales correctly.

Mindful Melodies

Bonus Action — begin performing a Melody targeting creatures of her choice within 30 ft.

Melody Rules

  • A creature stops being a target when it leaves range; when one enters the Melody's area she may make it a target (no action).
  • A Melody lasts 1 minute, though she can stop at any time (no action).
  • Melodies automatically stop if Sika is incapacitated or unable to speak.
  • Starting / switching a Melody is the Mindful Melodies bonus action and costs 5 MP.
  • She may start or switch a Melody at most once per turn (refreshed by New Turn on the console).
  • Because Alcoholic is also a bonus action, she can't drink and switch Melodies on the same turn — set the stance before she starts drinking.
  • Switching while Hymn of the Guardian temp HP is out causes that temp HP to decay by the Melody Factor each round.
While Wasted: her Melody is locked — she rides whatever tune she was performing and cannot switch. If it ends from a failed CON save she has no Melody and no hymns until Wasted passes. Pick her stance before the 4th drink.

Hymns Free Action / Reaction

  • Each Melody grants access to different hymns (religious prayers).
  • Once per turn, Sika can play one hymn as a free action.
  • Playing additional hymns the same turn, or playing one as a reaction, costs +3 MP.
  • She chooses who each hymn affects within the Melody's range.
How the console charges MP: the first hymn each turn spends only its listed cost; every extra hymn that turn adds +3 MP on top. Use New Turn on the Melodies tab to refresh the free hymn. (Interpretation of the +3 rule — confirm with your DM if you read it differently.)

Abilities

Psychic Linguist Passive

Sika can understand ANY creature while it is speaking — as if a text bubble pops up next to its head.

Masterful Performer Passive

When Sika performs a Melody she may also choose a Mystic Song to combine with it. The Mystic Song's effect happens simultaneously with the Melody.

Alcoholic Bonus Action

Sika takes a little alcohol, gaining:

  • +5 MP
  • Her CHA modifier is doubled for one round
  • An extra free hymn, lasting one round

Drinking more than three times sends her into the Wasted state (Dizzy). Her drink tally persists until a short or long rest — sobering up from Dizzy doesn't clear it, so she can't keep stacking fresh drinks without resting.

Resonant Mindpower Reaction

Sika lends her mental power to bolster others:

  • If a creature spends MP, she may give up to 10 of hers to that creature, or
  • Spend 3 MP to add her PB to a morale, fear, or sanity check.

Wasted Passive · High Risk / High Reward

After too much alcohol Sika becomes drunk:

  • She gains the Dizzy status effect.
  • Hymns cost no MP.
  • She can barely keep concentration: roll a CON save every round (DC 20, rising per hymn used). On a failure the Melody ends and she can't start another until the state ends.
  • Lasts 3 rounds. If she rides it out, Dizzy fades cleanly; if she drinks to extend it, the comedown is the Nauseous status — lasting as long as the bender.

Status Effects

The drunk progression is one ladder: Dizzy (Tier I) climbs to Nauseous (Tier II). Both are now in the handbook.

Dizzy Tier I · while drunk

Sika gains this on her 4th drink and it runs for 3 rounds:

  • The world tilts — disadvantage on Dexterity checks and on attacks made after moving over half her speed; speed reduced.
  • She can still take reactions.
  • Ride it out — if she lets the 3 rounds lapse without drinking again, Dizzy fades cleanly, no hangover.
  • Extend it — each drink she takes to stay drunk refreshes the 3-round clock and racks up a bender. Any bender means the comedown will be Nauseous, and each extension also raises the CON DC to hold her tune.

Refer to the handbook entry for the exact figures.

Nauseous Tier II · the hangover

Only an extended Dizzy turns to Nauseous when it ends — a hangover, not venom:

  • Cannot take reactions. Resonant Mindpower and Stunning Solo both go offline.
  • Speed halved; disadvantage on attacks and STR/DEX saves; the pounding head leaves her vulnerable to thunder & psychic (this replaces any poison tick — sensitivity, not toxin).
  • Healing received is halved while she can't keep anything down.
  • Lasts as long as the bender — the Nauseous duration equals the number of times she extended the Dizzy. A quick top-up is a short hangover; a long bender lingers. A rest clears it instantly.
  • No ongoing poison damage — the sick feeling is the cost, not a venom DOT.
Dial: halved healing bites a self-healer hardest — if it's too rough, limit it to potions/consumables and let her hymn-healing through. Match whatever you locked into the handbook.
