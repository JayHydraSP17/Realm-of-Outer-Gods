"Showcasing why he is called the Diamond in the Rough."

Yohan amplifies the momentum rules, turning motion into destructive force. Without proper positioning or strategy, he can find himself buried under the horizons. Two momentum tracks fuel everything — manage them on the Sands of Momentum tab.

Weapon & Equipment

Horizon’s Reach Halberd 1d12

A long-hafted desert glaive — the polearm of the Diamond in the Rough. Its length lets Yohan threaten the line a full stride before a foe expects it, perfect fuel for straight-line momentum.

  • Damage — 1d12 slashing (standard halberd; feeds Dunebreaker Strike’s slashing property)
  • Reach — +5 ft extra reach on top of normal weapon reach
  • Add Dune Stacks as bonus weapon damage dice (up to +6d12) when charging in a straight line.

Two Tracks of Momentum

Dune Momentum Straight Line

Follows JT’s Improved D&D. For every 10 ft moved in a straight line:

  • Gain +1 weapon damage die
  • Gain +1 Momentum stack
  • Maximum bonus: +6 for both stacks & damage dice.

Thresholds:

  • 1 stack — Knockback can be used to launch
  • 3 stacks — 1 Extra Attack
  • 6 stacks — Momentum Finisher: Auto-Critical & applies a weapon-based status

Sandstorm Momentum Distance

For every 10 ft of continuous movement, gain 1 Momentum Die (d8). Expands to d10 at 17th level.

  • Cannot retrace movement on the same turn (no back-and-forth)
  • Maximum Momentum Dice = PB
  • Can only generate 3 per round
  • All Momentum Dice vanish at end of turn unless preserved (Buried Momentum)

Passives

Hmph. Passive

If Yohan has Momentum Dice and a creature attempts to forcibly move him, he lowers the distance by 5 ft × MD.

Lone Sand Passive

When there is no ally within 20 ft, Yohan gains +2 AC.

Momentum Spenders

Dunebreaker Strike Free Action All MD

Once per turn, expend all Momentum Dice on one creature for bonus damage + a status based on the attack’s property:

  • Piercing — target loses AC equal to your PB
  • Slashing — target bleeds for additional MD damage at the start of its next turn
  • Bludgeoning — push target up to 20 ft

Shifting Sands Reaction 1 MD

When targeted by an attack, expend 1 MD to gain AC = PB against that attack.

  • If the attack misses, move 5 ft without provoking OAs — this movement does not expend stored Momentum.

Sunpiercer Descent Attack 2 MD

Jump up to 15 ft without provoking OAs. On hit:

  • Deal bonus radiant damage equal to MD
  • Target makes a CON save or is blinded

Dunewall Charge Charge 1+ MD

When charging in a straight line, expend 1 MD per creature in your path. Each makes a STR save:

  • Fail — pushed aside, knocked prone, takes MD damage
  • Success — may try to stop you with an OA
  • Interrupted if terrain changes; you must move up to half your speed before you can stop.

Buried Momentum Bonus Action

Plant your feet, entering a recursive loop. You retain all Momentum Dice generated during your turn.

  • Every 5 ft you move or are forcibly moved, lose 1 MD.
  • Toggle Buried on the Momentum Dice panel above.

Rising Dawn Bonus Action

Instantly gain 3 MD, but lose half your movement speed this turn.

Stances of the Desert Bonus Action · 3 Ki

Expend 3 Ki to activate a stance. It lasts until the start of your next turn. Only one stance is active at a time — activating a new one replaces the old.

Red Dunes ● Active

  • +10 movement speed
  • First creature hit takes bonus damage = MD expended this turn
  • Reduce a creature to 0 HP → gain 1 MD

Sirocco ● Active

  • Difficult terrain does not slow you
  • +5 AC against ranged attacks while moving
  • First 10 ft of movement each turn generates 1 free MD

Scorpion ● Active

  • Creatures within 10 ft who miss their attack provoke a free OA
  • Opportunity attacks deal bonus damage = 1 MD
  • If a creature misses an attack twice in one round, gain 1 MD

Buried Tomb ● Active

  • If you moved less than 15 ft this turn, gain resistance to all damage except psychic
  • Cannot be knocked prone or forcibly moved
  • Expend 1 MD to reduce damage by + PB

Reactions

Rolling Tumbleweed Reaction

When Yohan is forcibly moved, knocked prone, or falls, he may roll with the motion instead. He isn’t knocked prone.

  • For every 10 ft he travels this way, generate 1 Momentum Die (max of 3).
  • He may end in any space along the path.

Prickly Cactus Reaction

When a creature within 5 ft hits Yohan with a melee attack, he may expend 1 Momentum Die to bristle — the attacker takes piercing damage equal to 1 MD + PB.

  • While Buried Momentum is active, this costs no Momentum Die.

Sword Saint Techniques Save DC = 27

Blasting Spear Attack 2 Ki

On a piercing melee attack, spend 2 focus to forgo the roll. All creatures in a 30 ft long, 5 ft wide line make a DEX save, taking the weapon’s damage on a failure.

Majestic Bonus Action 2 Ki

Spend 2 focus as a bonus action: all attacks against you are made with disadvantage until the start of your next turn.

Thunder Joust Trigger 1 Ki

When you move 10+ ft toward a creature in a straight line, spend 1 focus for advantage on your next weapon attack vs them. On hit, if the target is no more than one size larger, it makes a STR save or is knocked prone.
